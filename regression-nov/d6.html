Cozy Camp D6 PreOrder Privacy Policy

This privacy policy explains how Test Org May 15 2024 12-27-21, the developer of the Cozy Camp D6 PreOrder app for Meta Quest, collects, uses and shares information about you when you use Cozy Camp D6 PreOrder. Contact: jahanbihamtagroup@gmail.com.

Information we collect

Cozy Camp D6 PreOrder may collect the following types of information: your Meta Horizon user ID, your display name and profile photo, your friends list (only friends who also own Cozy Camp D6 PreOrder), device information such as headset model and OS version, and usage data such as session length and in-game progress.

How we use information

We use this information to save your progress, show your name to other campers in shared sessions, let you invite friends, improve game performance, analyze player behavior in aggregate and enable in-app purchases. We do not sell your information and we do not use it for advertising.

Sharing

We share information only with service providers that host our servers, under contract, and when required by law.

How to delete your data

You can request deletion of any data we hold about you at any time, from any country, free of charge, by emailing jahanbihamtagroup@gmail.com with the subject 'Delete my Cozy Camp D6 PreOrder data'. We delete the data within 30 days and confirm by email.
