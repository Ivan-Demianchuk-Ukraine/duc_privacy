App_3 Privacy Policy

This privacy policy explains how Hex Team (Jahan Bihamta VR), the developer of the App_3 app for Meta Quest, collects, uses and shares information about you when you use App_3. Contact: jahanbihamtagroup@gmail.com.

Contents and summary of your rights

1. Information we collect. 2. How we use information. 3. Sharing. 4-12. Detailed processing notices for each game mode. 13. Retention. 14. Deleting your data. 15. Contact.

Summary of your rights: you can ask us to access, correct or delete the information we hold about you. How to send a deletion request, what we delete and how long it takes are described in Section 14 (Deleting your data) at the end of this policy.

Information we collect

App_3 may collect the following types of information: your Meta Horizon user ID, your display name and profile photo, your friends list (only friends who also own App_3), device information such as headset model and OS version, and usage data such as session length and in-game progress.

How we use information

We use this information to save your progress, show your name to other campers in shared sessions, let you invite friends, improve game performance, analyze player behavior in aggregate and enable in-app purchases. We do not sell your information and we do not use it for advertising.

Sharing

We share information only with service providers that host our servers, under contract, and when required by law.
